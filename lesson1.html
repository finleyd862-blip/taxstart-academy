Lesson 1: What Is Income Tax?

Welcome to your first Tax Star Academy lesson. Before learning how to prepare a tax return, you need to understand what income tax is and how the basic system works.

Income tax: A tax imposed by the government on certain types of income received by individuals and businesses.

1. Gross Income

Gross income generally refers to income received before certain adjustments, deductions, or other tax calculations.

Examples can include wages, salaries, business income, interest, dividends, and other taxable sources of income.

2. Taxable Income

Taxable income is generally the amount of income that remains subject to tax after applicable adjustments and deductions are considered.

Important: Not every dollar a person receives is necessarily taxed in the same way. Tax treatment depends on the type of income and the applicable tax rules.

3. Tax Deductions

A deduction can reduce the amount of income subject to tax. Taxpayers may qualify for different deductions depending on their circumstances.

4. Tax Credits

A tax credit generally reduces the amount of tax owed rather than reducing taxable income.

5. Tax Refunds

A refund can occur when the amount of tax already paid or withheld is greater than the taxpayer's final tax liability, subject to the applicable rules.

Tax Star Tip: Never assume that a tax refund means someone paid "no taxes." A refund is generally the result of comparing payments or withholding with the taxpayer's final liability.
